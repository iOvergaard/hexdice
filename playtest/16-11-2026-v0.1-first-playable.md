## Findings, worst first

1. **Reinforcements are invisible.** After End turn, my 3 new dice landed on two territories and the numbers just changed. Nothing shows where they went or why 3. The controller doesn't animate the `reinforced` event. Fix: pulse the territories that received dice with a "+N", and say "You +3 (largest group 3)" in the roll strip; include the knockout bonus and reserve when non-zero.
2. **Board too small on a phone.** At 375×812 the landscape board is about 350×160 px; tokens are barely legible and the bottom 40% of the screen is empty. Fix: rotate the board for portrait screens (same map and seed, flat-top hexes), so it fills the height.
3. **No "your turn" cue.** The last AI roll stays in the strip when your turn starts. Fix: clear the strip and show "Your turn: tap one of your territories".
4. **Preview doesn't say which colour you are.** The player bar is hidden on the preview. Fix: "You are ● [colour]" next to "Play this board?".
5. **Knockout bonus isn't visible.** The player bar shows largest group and reserve but not the per-turn bonus. Fix: show "+N/turn" on the chip when non-zero.
6. **Seat bias (balance, from the simulator).** In 8-player games the last two seats win about 16–17% each vs the expected 12.5%. Logged for a later balance pass.
