# Upright phones: the game fits the screen (23-11-2026)

Fixes [#29](https://github.com/iOvergaard/hexdice/issues/29), "On an upright iPhone, End turn starts half off the screen". Jacob picked option 1: let the board take what's left on an upright phone instead of a fixed minimum, and pad for the safe-area insets.

## What was wrong

Below 720 px wide the game screen had `min-height: 100dvh` and the board `min-height: 320px`, so whenever the roll strip (96 px), the board (320) and the player bar didn't fit, the page grew past the screen. In Safari with its bars an iPhone 13 shows 390×664 (Playwright's profile). With 8 players the player bar is 257 px (two rows of chips, the controls row, End turn), the game 717 px tall, and End turn's bottom 39 of its 52 px sat below the screen. At 320×568 the narrow chips stack their emblem over the numbers, the bar is 367 px, and End turn sat 245 px below the screen. The e2e phone, a Pixel 7 at 412×839, has room, so no test caught it.

![Before: iPhone 13, 8 players, End turn cut off](screens/23-11-2026-phone-fit-before-390x664.png)

There was also no `env(safe-area-inset-*)` padding on the upright game screen or the title. The manifest asks for `standalone` and the page uses `viewport-fit=cover`, so on an iPhone home-screen install the home indicator overlapped End turn's bottom edge.

## The change

CSS only, in `src/ui/ui.css`:

- `.game` is exactly one screen tall (`height: 100dvh`) and the board, `flex: 1`, takes what the roll strip and player bar leave, down to `min-height: 240px` (was 320).
- `min-height: min-content` on `.game`: when even 240 px won't fit, the page grows and scrolls as before, and End turn keeps its 14 px of padding below it. With `height: 100dvh` alone, End turn would sit flush with the end of the page.
- Wide screens (720 px and up) keep `height: 100dvh` with no minimum, as before. The landscape block at the end of the file overrides all of this for phones on their side, untouched.
- `.game` and `.title` pad with `max(<old padding>, env(safe-area-inset-*))` on each side, as the landscape block already does.

Seeds and saves are unaffected.

### How big the map is at 390×664

`BoardView.layout()` turns the map when its box is taller than wide. At 390×664 with 8 players the box goes from 358×320 to 358×267: wider than tall both times, so no map turns. The map is scaled to fit the box, and most continents are wide enough to be limited by the 358 px width either way. Measured over seeds 1 to 80, 8 players, the old CSS injected for "before":

- 65 of 80 maps keep exactly their size.
- 7 shrink by under 3% (seeds 4, 18, 32, 60, 68, 77, 79).
- 8 tall continents shrink by 7.5 to 16.5% (seeds 27, 35, 45, 48, 62, 64, 73, 74). Seed 101 is one too: 351×289 px → 311×256 px, hex radius 6.15 → 5.45 px.
- Mean change −1.4%.

With 2 to 4 players the bar has one row of chips and the board keeps its 341 px at this size.

### Later in a game

A knockout bonus adds a "+1/turn" line to a chip, and a chip with a reserve and a bonus wraps onto a third line, so the player bar grows during a game. I autoplayed whole 8-player games and watched the page on every change to the bar:

| 390×664 | 202, 42, 12, 7 | 295 px (one bonus line per chip row) | 11 px | 91 px | 661, wholly on screen |
| 390×664 | 101 | 314 px (round 5) | 30 px | 110 px | 680, its bottom 16 px below the screen |
| 375×667 | 202, 101, 42 | 295 to 314 px | 8 to 27 px | 88 to 107 px | 661 to 680 |
| 360×640 | 202, 101, 42 | 295 to 314 px | 35 to 54 px | 115 to 134 px | 661 to 680 |
| 390×664, 2 players | 202, 303, 42 | 202 px | 0 | | 650 |

![Round 4, seed 101: two bonus lines push the page 11 px; End turn is still on screen](screens/23-11-2026-phone-fit-mid-game-bonus.png)

So End turn starts wholly on screen. At 390×664 the page starts to scroll once the bar grows by more than the board's 27 px to spare, and End turn's bottom leaves the screen past 41 px (its 14 px of padding goes first). **For Jacob:** a lower floor would hold it. With 200 px instead of 240 (tried by injecting the rule), the same five games at 390×664 never scrolled, and the board went down to 210 px when the bar was tallest. I kept the 240 px that was asked for.

### The map preview

At 390×664 the preview prompt replaces the player bar, so the board is 358×407 and the map is turned. After Yes the bar comes back and the board is 358×267 (358×320 before this change), so the map turns back and shrinks by a little more than before. This is the second finding in [the 22-11-2026 report](22-11-2026-random-games.md), which a separate branch is fixing (the prompt keeping the bar's height). Once both are merged, the preview's board should match the game's, 267 px.

## Gates

- `npm run check`: tsc passes. Vitest: 237 passed with `--maxWorkers=2`. With the default workers, `tests/unit/ai.test.ts` "only returns legal actions" (200 AI games, 3.5 s on its own) went past its 5 s timeout while other sessions loaded the machine (load average 5 to 13 on 4 cores). This change touches no file Vitest runs.
- `npm run e2e`: 129 passed, 14 skipped (desktop-only or phone-only tests) and 1 failed, on desktop (1280×800) and Pixel 7. The failure was the desktop "a resumed game's ending still covers the moves before the reload": the autoplayed game didn't finish within 90 s under load. Re-run, `game-over.spec.ts` passed on both projects. Run against the preinstalled Chromium through a scratch config with `executablePath`, because this container's browser build (1243) is older than the one `@playwright/test` 1.65.1 expects.
- `sim`: not needed, no rule or AI change.
