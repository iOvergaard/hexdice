# Music (27-11-2026)

Music with its own toggle, off until the player switches it on. The tracks are all by RandomMind, CC0 on OpenGameArt, chosen by Jacob:

| When | Track | File | Length |
|---|---|---|---|
| Through the game | The Bard's Tale (loops) | `public/music/bards-tale.m4a`, 1.26 MB | 2:39 |
| Two players left, in a game that started with three or more | Battle (loops) | `battle.m4a`, 0.62 MB | 1:19 |
| You win | Victory Theme (once) | `victory.m4a`, 0.26 MB | 0:32 |
| You lose, or you're knocked out | Defeat Theme (once) | `defeat.m4a`, 0.36 MB | 0:45 |

- **Files.** The original MP3s (7.8 MB) were turned into mono AAC at 64 kbit/s with `afconvert`. Plain gain first, so all four sit at about −23.5 LUFS (measured with ffmpeg's `ebur128`); the originals ran from −17.6 to −9.6. Total 2.5 MB.
- **Playback.** Tracks play on the sound's AudioContext, so the speaker toggle and the iOS silent switch silence them too. Loops fade in over 2 s and crossfade on a change; the one-off themes come in over 0.3 s and don't restart. The music dips under every dice roll: to 45% under yours, to 70% under the computer players' quieter rolls. The title screen is silent.
- **Offline.** Tracks aren't in the precache, so they don't slow down "Ready to play offline". The service worker keeps each track in a `hexdice-music` cache the first time it plays, and keeps that cache across deploys.
- **Phone layout.** The game bar needed 36 px more at 375 px. On phones the icon buttons are now 40 px wide (as in the landscape column), gaps 3 px, Quit's padding 6 px, and the speed menu shrinks first. On the title the extras row uses 6 px gaps and 10 px padding, so How to play and What's new stay on one line.
