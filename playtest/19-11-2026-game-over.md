# Game over screen (19-11-2026)

An art-director review on 19-11-2026 found the end of a game underplayed: "Cinder wins" was a thin dark band across the board, with the same weight as the "Atlas eliminated" callout. The winner's colour barely showed and nothing explained how the win happened.

**The change.** The band is now a card. A header in the winner's colour carries their emblem, the title ("You win", "Grove wins", "You were eliminated") and how long it took. Under it, a chart of the territories each player held, round by round, stacked in finishing order with the winner at the bottom, so their colour fills the box as they take the map. A mark above the chart shows where each player went out. A line under the chart is about your game: if you won, your biggest turn and who you knocked out; if you lost, how big you got, who took your last territory and where you came. Replay, Title and Watch the rest keep their places and test ids. On a phone the card covers the whole screen, because the board area alone is too short for it.

Jacob picked this from three directions (screenshots in the session notes, `game-over-directions.png`): A, the winner's colour leads with a finishing-order list; B, the territories chart; C, a win that takes the whole board and a loss that steps aside. He chose A's header with B's chart. C's stamp survives as the one difference between winning and losing: your emblem stamps in when you win.

**Where the numbers come from.** `src/game/summary.ts` reads the event log from the state it started at: territory counts per round, places, who knocked out whom, each player's peak, biggest turn and least likely won attack. The UI decides no rules. A resumed game used to start an empty event log, so its summary would have missed everything before the reload; `restore()` now hands back the events its replay produced.

**One change after the pick.** The chart Jacob saw took a sample at every turn. In a two-player endgame that zig-zags with every turn and hides the trend, so it now samples once per round (`game-over-sampling.png`: per turn on top, per round below). The peak in the caption still counts every capture.
