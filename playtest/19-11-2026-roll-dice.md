# Roll strip dice as cubes (19-11-2026)

The board's dice became cubes on 18-11-2026, but the roll strip at the top still drew flat ink squares with a coloured outline and coloured pips, so a roll looked like it came from another game (art review, 19-11-2026). The strip now draws each rolled die as the same cube as the top of a board stack.

**Branch:** `feat/roll-dice` (worktree `.claude/worktrees/roll-dice`), off main `fe33510`.
**Played by:** Claude, in headless Chromium at 1280×800 @2x and 375×812 @3x (emulated phone), scripted against a dev server of the worktree.

## What changed

- **Cube dice:** `dieFace()` in `src/ui/roll-view.ts` draws `cubeDie()` from `src/ui/units.ts`, the board's own `die()`: front face in the player colour with the pips, the 45% darker side, the top mixed with the cool `#dfe8ff` tint, the thin ink seam. The strip's own pip table and the `.die-plate` / `.die-pip` styles are gone.
- **Shared colours:** each roll side sets `--c`, `--cd` and `--pip` with `playerColour`, `darken` and `pipColour` from `src/ui/identity.ts`, as the board does, and the dice use the board's `.u-face`, `.u-side`, `.u-top` and `.u-pip` classes. Nothing is copied.
- **Size:** the front face is as wide as before on desktop (about 23 px) and about 13 px on a phone. On a phone each cube tucks its side under the next one, so 8 dice fit the 119 px a side has at 375 px. On desktop the cubes stand apart.
- **Kept:** the tumble animation, `prefers-reduced-motion`, `role="img"`, `data-value` and `aria-label` on each die.
- **Harness:** `harness.html?roll=1&dice=8x8` rolls that many dice a side, for screenshots and the new e2e test.

## Open for Jacob

1. **Smaller dice for short rolls on a phone.** The old 16 px dice were squeezed to about 13 px when 8 were rolled, but kept 16 px (pips 2.9 px) for shorter rolls. The cubes are one size for every roll, sized so 8 fit: pips about 2.2 px. The big sum still carries the result. Growing the dice for short rolls would make the size change from roll to roll.
2. **Winner box hangs below the strip on a phone (not from this change).** When an attacker wins with a two-digit total, the ✓ wraps to its own line and the highlighted box hangs about 17 px below the strip, over the board. It happens with the old dice too (see the "before" column above).
